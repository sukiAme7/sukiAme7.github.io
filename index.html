About

I am a master's student in Artificial Intelligence at Nankai University, where I began my studies in 2025. My research interests include spatial reasoning, vision-language models, and depth estimation.

Research Interests

I study how intelligent systems represent spatial relationships, understand scenes, and reason about objects, positions, directions, and distance. My work explores the connection between vision-language models and spatial understanding.

  • Vision-language models
  • Depth estimation
  • Scene understanding
